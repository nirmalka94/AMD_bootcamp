# Day 8 — Mock Interview and Consolidation

**Theme:** Demonstrate independent C++ problem solving and explain robotics software design under interview conditions.

This is not a new theory day. Use the day to consolidate the preceding seven days and practise communicating clearly.

## Learning outcomes
- Solve a medium-sized C++ problem under time pressure.
- Clarify requirements and edge cases before coding.
- Explain complexity, ownership, lifetime, and concurrency decisions.
- Discuss a robotics software pipeline with concrete engineering trade-offs.
- Identify remaining gaps and create a focused follow-up plan.

## Schedule (about 5.5 focused hours)
| Block | Duration | Activity |
|---|---:|---|
| 1 | 15 min | Review personal weak areas |
| 2 | 60 min | Timed coding round |
| 3 | 20 min | Review and explain solution |
| 4 | 15 min | Break |
| 5 | 60 min | Two challenges (LRU cache, concurrency design) |
| 6 | 50 min | C++/systems technical interview |
| 7 | 45 min | Robotics architecture discussion |
| 8 | 45 min | Final cumulative exam |
| 9 | 15 min | Gap analysis and close |

## Ground rules
- No AI assistant, autocomplete-generated solution, or internet search during timed exercises.
- Use a compiler and minimal local tests.
- Speak your reasoning aloud.
- If stuck, state what you know, propose a simpler correct approach, and continue.
