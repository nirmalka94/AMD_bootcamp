# Day 8 Implementation — Mock Interview Protocol

## Round 1 — Timed coding (60 minutes)
Use `starter/mock_coding.cpp`:
```bash
cd starter
g++ -std=c++17 -Wall -Wextra -Wpedantic mock_coding.cpp -o mock_coding
./mock_coding
```
Write and run your own test cases first; the self-checks at the end of `main` are a final confirmation.

Suggested protocol:
- 5 minutes: clarify requirements and examples.
- 10 minutes: outline brute force and optimised approach.
- 35 minutes: implement and test.
- 10 minutes: explain complexity and improvements.

Do not open the solution until the round is complete.

## Challenges (60 minutes)
Complete both drills in `CHALLENGES.md` under timed conditions.

## Round 2 — C++ and systems discussion (50 minutes)
Use the prompts in `TECHNICAL_INTERVIEW.md`. Answer aloud, not by reading notes. Keep answers concise and use examples.

## Round 3 — Robotics architecture (45 minutes)
Choose one robotics project you have worked on. Explain:
- system requirements and constraints;
- module boundaries and data flow;
- concurrency and ownership;
- performance bottleneck and how it was measured;
- failure modes and recovery;
- design trade-offs and what you would change.

## Final exam (45 minutes)
Complete `EXAM.md` under timed conditions.

## Final output
Create a one-page personal gap list:
- three topics you can explain confidently;
- three topics that need another practice session;
- two coding patterns to practise;
- one project story to prepare in depth.
